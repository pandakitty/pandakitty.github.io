The "What": Technical Methodology

Using Python and unsupervised learning techniques, I built a behavioral segmentation model utilizing **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)** scoring combined with **K-Means clustering**. This approach groups customers based on their exact transaction habits—highlighting how recently they bought, how often they engage, and total monetary contribution—without manual categorization overhead.

Segment MetricStrategic Focus
High-Value ChampionsVIP rewards, early access, and loyalty programs
At-Risk AccountsAutomated re-engagement and targeted incentives

The "Why": Business Analytics Impact

Treating all customers uniformly wastes marketing budget and misses churn signals. By translating complex clustering outputs into distinct behavioral buckets, marketing and leadership teams can deploy precise campaigns that protect revenue and maximize lifetime value.
